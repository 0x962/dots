# Steps 

## Gather PR Context 

1. What PR are we on right now? Is all the code pushed up to the branch? Have we pulled in from the target branch to ensure the diff is accurate? 
2. This PR may be a part of a stack. Are there other PRs above or below this one in the stack that we should look at as well? 

## Gather Product Context

1. What product are we working on? For example, is it the "operator" on Canary? 
2. What specific feature or subset of the product are we working on? For example, is it the chat or routines feature? 
3. Who are the users? How is the product used? 

## Gather Business Context

1. What mode are we functioning in? Are we trying to get it right or are we trying to ship fast?

## Establish Risk 

1. Are we modifying a critical production codepath? 
2. Are there active users?
3. Are there database or infrastructure changes involved? 
4. Is there risk of downtime? 
5. Is this change backwards compatible? 

## Open Ended

1. Your job is to be excellent at your job. Do not act like a task taker. 
2. Your responsibility is to deliver a good, concise and complete summary that enables every other agent in this workflow. 
3. Exercise freedom and include or exclude additional context that will help other agents. 

## Self Improve 

1. Save your learnings to memory so you get better over time.
